## ✨ About the project

SPORTCLIP is a hands-free workout companion built into a secure, sporty hair clip.

It guides you through every session with a warm, motivating voice, so you can focus on moving instead of watching a screen or following a video.

Made to bring more freedom, comfort and encouragement to your workouts.
##  The problem

During a workout, following a video or constantly checking a phone can interrupt movement and take attention away from the exercise itself.

Switching between watching a screen, listening to instructions and performing movements can make a workout less fluid and enjoyable.

SPORTCLIP aims to solve this problem by offering a hands-free audio experience, allowing users to focus entirely on their movements while receiving clear instructions and encouragement throughout their session.
## ⚙️ How it works

### Version 1 — Demo prototype

For the first version, developed as part of Project Fallen, SPORTCLIP will feature one pre-recorded workout session.

Due to the project's time constraints, this initial version will focus on demonstrating the core concept and its hands-free audio guidance system.

In future versions, SPORTCLIP is intended to connect to a dedicated mobile application, allowing users to choose between multiple workout sessions and personalize their experience.

### The workout experience

1. **Start:** The user presses the button to launch the session.
2. **Welcome:** SPORTCLIP plays a warm welcome message and a short motivational introduction.
3. **Session introduction:** The voice announces the workout, its exercises and their durations.
4. **Countdown:** A countdown signals the beginning of the session.
5. **Exercise guidance:** The voice announces each exercise, its duration and provides spoken countdowns during the final five seconds.
6. **Rest and transitions:** SPORTCLIP announces rest periods and guides the user through the transition to the next exercise.
7. **Encouragement:** Motivational messages accompany the workout to help the user stay engaged.
8. **Finish:** At the end of the session, SPORTCLIP congratulates the user, celebrates their effort and ends with a friendly message inviting them to come back for another workout.
